# Crafting research routing

For Path of Exile 2 pasted-item crafting requests, read `docs/poe2-crafting-engine/WORKFLOW.md` and `docs/poe2-crafting-engine/CRAFTING_MECHANICS.md`. Research and save the requested class's modifier pool and current league prices before claiming a cheapest route. Follow session preferences; do not inherit PoE1 league, budget or acquisition defaults. Preserve exact requested tiers and report missing evidence rather than inventing probabilities.

For Path of Exile 1, continue using `docs/crafting-engine/CRAFTING_MECHANICS.md` and its references. The executable `crafting-engine/` is PoE1; `poe2-crafting-engine/` is separate. Do not cross-load their data.
